About Me

I'm Violet Guo (Xiaowei Guo), a game designer currently at Petch, designing personality-driven social PvP systems. Before that I built multi-agent narrative systems at Dramai.AI (Cuboid.AI), tuned combat AI on Game of Thrones: Winter is Coming at NetEase, and wrote character arcs for Revelation. MS in Game Design, Northeastern University.

I care about systems that stay legible as they grow, and stories that emerge from play instead of cutscenes. The same craft carries into my AI-native and product work — I design multi-agent experiences, and ship mobile products end to end, from Figma to React Native.

Based in Boston, also at home in Hangzhou. I work in English and Chinese.
